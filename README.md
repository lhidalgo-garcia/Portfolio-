# Welcome to My Portfolio

This portfolio showcases academic and applied projects focused on data analysis, business decision-making, and geospatial analysis through Management Information Systems coursework.


## Projects

### GIS Project: Frozen Yogurt
- Conducted GIS-based analysis to evaluate optimal business locations for a frozen yogurt franchise using mapping tools and competitor data
- Identified underserved markets in Santa Ana, Newport Beach, and Costa Mesa by comparing demand and competition levels to Irvine
- Evaluated geographic accessibility, market saturation, and demographic indicators to support data-driven location recommendations
- Developed and presented findings through a StoryMap visualization tool
- View the StoryMap link: https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/dae42c17584849169b0d6450c24fb0da
